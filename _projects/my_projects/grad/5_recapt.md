## Overview

As part of an **entrepreneurship course at KTH Royal Institute of Technology**, our team explored the challenges people face when tracking everyday expenses.

We conducted **user surveys and interviews** to understand existing spending habits and pain points, and developed **Recapt**, a mobile expense-tracking app designed to make recording purchases faster and more intuitive.

The app uses **receipt image recognition** to extract purchase information automatically, reducing the need for manual data entry. Spending data is then presented through visual charts to help users understand their spending patterns.

The project combined **user research, problem validation, product ideation, and software development** to develop and pitch a potential product.

## User Research

Before developing the application, we conducted **surveys and user research** to understand how people currently track their expenses and identify common pain points.

Our research highlighted the friction involved in **manually recording individual purchases**, motivating us to explore a solution that could automate this process while still providing users with useful insights into their spending.

## Solution

Based on our research, we proposed **Recapt**, a mobile application that simplifies expense tracking through receipt scanning.

Users can capture a receipt using the app, after which the relevant purchase information is extracted and added to their expense history. The application organizes the information and presents spending patterns through visual charts.

## Product Development

The project followed an iterative entrepreneurship process:

**User Research → Problem Identification → Ideation → Prototyping → Validation → Product Pitch**

Rather than starting with the technology, we used **survey results and user feedback** to identify the problem and shape the proposed solution.

We then developed a functional prototype to demonstrate the core user journey and communicate how automated receipt processing could simplify everyday expense management.
